Most games escape the office. Ours walk back in.

## The premise

Work already feels like a game with bad rules. Meetings spawn. Energy bars deplete. Bosses drop loot that is mostly calendar invites. So we make that visible.

**职场魔塔** turns the career ladder into a tower. **办公室射击** is survival comedy. **咖啡游戏** is the ritual that keeps you human.

## Not satire only

Yes, there is satire. There is also care. The coffee game is not a joke about caffeine — it is about reclaiming three quiet minutes.

Games can be tiny. They can be unfinished. They can still tell the truth about a Tuesday afternoon.

If you have ever wanted to fight a printer, you already understand the genre.
