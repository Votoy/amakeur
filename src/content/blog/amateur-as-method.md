Amateur comes from *amare* — to love. Somewhere along the way it became an insult.

We took the word back for **AMAKEUR**. Not as cosplay humility. As a method: stay close enough to the work to still feel embarrassed by bad taste.

## 野造

野造 is the Chinese half of the name. Wild-making. A path that is not the official trail, but still leads somewhere.

Together: lover of the craft + wild path. Soft on ego. Hard on quality.

## What we refuse

- Marketing that sounds like a model wrote it for a model.
- Portfolios that glow for no reason.
- Features that exist to prove we can code.

We would rather ship a quiet notes app that respects privacy than a dashboard that impresses LinkedIn.

Amateur as method means: keep the love visible. Let the seriousness show in the details.
