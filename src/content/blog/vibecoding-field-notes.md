Vibecoding is not magic. It is pair-programming with something that forgets, hallucinates, and occasionally invents a better idea than yours.

## Rules that survived contact

**1. Taste before tokens.**  
If you cannot describe what “good” feels like, the model will invent a default — usually a card stack with purple gradients.

**2. Constrain the surface.**  
One accent. Two radii. A spacing scale. Boredom in the system creates freedom in the idea.

**3. Read every line you ship.**  
You do not have to write the code by hand. You do have to own it.

**4. Archive without shame.**  
Half of Labs is meant to die. The point is the learning, not the launch tweet.

## Why Labs exists

Some ideas do not fit Apps, Games, or Web. They are still worth making. Labs is the room where categories fail — and that is the feature.
